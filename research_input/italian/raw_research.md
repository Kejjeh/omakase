# NYC Italian Restaurants — Curated Dataset (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx)

## TL;DR
- This is a curated dataset of **156 Italian restaurants** across Manhattan (68), Brooklyn (47), Queens (24), and Bronx (17), confirmed operating in 2026, mixing institutions (Rao's, Bamonte's, Mario's, Gargiulo's, L&B Spumoni Gardens, Don Peppe, Park Side) with current critical darlings (Lilia, Misi, Don Angie, San Sabino, Torrisi, Rezdôra, Roscioli, I Cavallini) and recent 2024–2026 openings (Borgo, San Sabino, Sempre Oggi, Briscola Trattoria, Bar Ferdinando, Massara).
- Densest clusters: **Arthur Avenue/Belmont (Bronx)**, **Carroll Gardens Court/Smith corridor (Brooklyn)**, **Williamsburg Bedford/Grand pasta belt**, **West Village Grove/Greenwich/Hudson axis**, **Astoria 30th Ave / Ditmars**, **Howard Beach Crossbay Blvd**.
- Michelin Italian footprint (2025 Guide): **1 star** — Rezdôra; **Bib Gourmand** — Don Angie, Via Carota, Roberta's, Una Pizza Napoletana, Café Mars, Burrata, Olmo (new), Via Vai; **Recommended** — Tra di Noi, Patricia's of Morris Park, Sempre Oggi (new), Torrisi, L'Artusi, Misi, Lilia, Carbone, San Sabino, Massara, I Cavallini, Borgo. **Flagged closed/excluded**: Artie's (City Island, closed Sept 2025), Tony & Tina's (Arthur Ave), Cafe al Mercato, MangiPasta, Il Buco (verify status — Time Out removed from list 2025).

## Key Findings
1. NYC Italian dining in 2026 is bifurcated: the legacy red-sauce institutions (20+ years) remain robust and add roughly half the dataset, while a **2018–2026 wave** of pasta-bar concepts (Lilia, Misi, Rezdôra, Forsythia, Massara, Cremini's, I Cavallini, San Sabino, Borgo, Briscola) has pushed handmade-pasta programs into the city's mainstream.
2. **Reservation difficulty** is the most reliable proxy for current heat — Carbone, Torrisi, Don Angie, Lilia, Via Carota, I Sodi, Rezdôra, San Sabino, Lucali, and I Cavallini are all "hard-to-book" with 7–30-day release windows.
3. **AVPN-certified Neapolitan**: Kesté, Ribalta, Don Antonio, Song' E Napule (confirmed by AVPN listings); Roberta's, Una Pizza Napoletana, Lucali, Juliana's, L'industrie are Neapolitan-influenced but **not AVPN-certified**.
4. The **Bronx and Queens are under-represented in most editorial lists** — Arthur Avenue alone supports a dozen institutional Italians (Roberto's, Mario's, Dominick's, Zero Otto Nove, Tra di Noi, Enzo's, Ann & Tony's, Antonio's, Pasquale's Rigoletto, Emilia's); Queens' Holy Trinity is **Park Side (Corona), Trattoria L'Incontro (Astoria), Don Peppe (S. Ozone Park)**.
